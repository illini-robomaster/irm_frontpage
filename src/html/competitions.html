2026 Purdue Spooky SCRIM.

Oct 31 – Nov 1 at Purdue’s Stewart Center. Our first field test of the season, run under the 2026 ARC Championship ruleset.

Departure · Fri Oct 30

8:30 AM

Loading starts 7:30 AM at the ECEB lab. Vehicles leave at 8:30 AM sharp — be there or make your own way to West Lafayette.

Return · Sun Nov 1

Afternoon

Matches end at noon, then field teardown. All borrowed referee system modules must be returned before we leave.

Before you go

Requirements.

The host published the field configuration. Robots that do not match it will not pass inspection.

  • Purdue is not providing referee system modules — bring your own; anything lent on site must be returned
  • Firmware versions must match the host’s published list (flash from the rmtool2 folder)
  • Modified 2025 RMUL server: VTM02 transmitter, VTM12 receivers — verify your link before leaving Urbana
  • Drive teams review the 2026 ARC ruleset and the 2025 MWC 2v2 field before Oct 30
  • Roster, transport, and module assignments go through your sub-team lead

Questions: illinirobomasters@gmail.com or the team Discord. Venue: 128 Memorial Mall Dr, West Lafayette, IN.
